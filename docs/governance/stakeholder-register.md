## 1. Stakeholder Classification & Roles

This register identifies the official project participants, their responsibilities, interest level, influence, and primary communication expectations. In accordance with privacy and academic guidelines, personal information uses explicit placeholders.

| Stakeholder Role | Classification | Core Responsibilities | Interest | Influence | Contact Information |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| **Project Lead / Lead Architect** | Technical & Execution Lead | Overall architectural integrity, code standards enforcement, database schema design, RBAC boundaries, and CI pipeline setup. | High | High | `[To be provided / Student Developer]` |

| **System Administrator Proxy** | Governance Proxy | Validates administrative account controls, global catalog maintenance, user management, and audit log utility. | Medium | Low | `[To be provided / Admin Proxy]` |
